# ShipPa

Ship public address. Every PA speaker anchored to a grid is part of that ship's network; there is nothing to wire.
Pilots use the PA panel on the shuttle console to set the situation code, sound general quarters, make announcements
and play internet sounds, and the collision warning sounds through it too. Speakers need power and distort when
damaged; a ship without speakers announces through its air alarms. Admins have `shippa_announce`, `shippa_code`,
`shippa_gq` and `shippa_speakers`.

Entry points: `ShipPaSystem` (speakers, broadcasts, alarms), `ShipAlertSystem` (situation codes, general quarters and
announcements from the console), `ShipAlarmPanel` (the console panel), the client `ShipPaMeshSystem` (plays the ship's
programme from real speaker positions), and `ShipAlertCodePrototype` for the codes.
