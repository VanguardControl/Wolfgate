# DeviceNetGuard

Keeps a device network packet storm from freezing the server. Upstream sends every queued packet in the tick it is
due, however many there are; on 2026-11-11 atmos alarms fed each other about three times more packets every tick
until one tick took over two minutes and the watchdog killed the server.

`DeviceNetworkSystem.GuardedUpdate` sends packets for at most `wf.device_net_guard.tick_budget_ms` per tick (after a
minimum of `min_packets_per_tick`, so ordinary traffic is never delayed) and leaves the rest, in order, for later
ticks. If the backlog passes `max_backlog` it is dropped whole and logged as an error naming the five devices that
queued most of it and their grids; repeat storms within a minute are only counted into the next report.
`wf.device_net_guard.enabled` turns the guard off.
