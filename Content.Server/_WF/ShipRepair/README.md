# ShipRepair

Wolfgate's rules for Monolith's ship repair device (SRD) on broken hulls. When a hull splits, in a planet crash or
under fire, the biggest piece stays the hull and keeps the one repair snapshot; every other piece is a section linked
back to it (`WFHullSectionComponent`), so there is one blueprint and nothing rebuilds on top of itself.

Using the SRD on a section reattaches it: it needs a full charge and uses all of it, the section within 32 tiles of
its place, that place clear and joined to the hull. A do-after of 5 s plus 0.1 s a tile (30 s at most) merges it back
at its own tile indices with everything aboard, decals, air and engines included. The SRD won't rebuild the hull under
another grid or where a section within range belongs. If the hull is deleted, its largest section on the same map
takes over the snapshot. A click works on the hull or section under it, so the SRD works from planet ground, and
admin-spawned vessels get a snapshot like bought ones.

Entry points: `WFHullSectionSystem` (target pick, rebuild guard, reattach checks), `WFHullSectionServerSystem` (split
links, hand-on, the merge) and the marked edits in `SharedShipRepairSystem.Tool` and `AdminVesselSpawnSystem`.
