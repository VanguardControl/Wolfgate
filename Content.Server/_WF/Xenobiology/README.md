# Xenobiology

Slime xenobiology, ported from Starlight (ss14Starlight/space-station-14, `_Starlight/Xenobiology`). Slimes eat
monkeys, split into four when well fed and sometimes mutate into another colour; mutation and stabilizer potions
steer the odds, and a slime at a 100% chance always splits into rainbow slimes. Dead slimes next to a powered slime
processor get pulled in and processed into extracts (four each, plus one per steroid potion). An extract reacts to
water, blood, plasma or radium in its solution: it makes reagents, potions, materials, gas, mobs or items depending
on its colour.

The xenobiology console sends out a remote eye that moves within 10 tiles of it. Through the eye the user picks up and
puts down one slime at a time, drops monkeys from stored cubes, recycles eaten monkeys into half cubes, scans slimes and
applies stored mutation or stabilizer potions. Stand next to the console to keep the eye open.

Players get in through the Xenobiology research tech (console and processor boards on the circuit imprinter, scanner
and extract bag on the protolathe) and the xenobiology starter crate from cargo (two gray slimes, a scanner and a bag).

Entry points: `SlimeSystem` (eating, splitting), `SlimeExtractReactionSystem` (extract reactions),
`SlimeProcessorSystem` and `SlimeProcessorUpdateSystem`, `XenobiologyConsoleSystem` (stored cubes and potions, the
leashed eye and its actions), the potion systems under `Potions/`, and the slime HTN operators under `NPC/`.

## Differences from Starlight

- The console's eye is Wolfgate's own. Starlight's RemoteEye console and tagged camera network don't exist here, so the
  eye spawns at the console, is pulled back onto a 10-tile leash and closes when the user walks off or loses power.
  The camera tagger is not ported.
- Wolfgate is on the old entity effects API, so the extract effects are rewritten as `SlimeSpawnEntity`,
  `SlimeSpawnFromTable`, `SlimeAddReagent`, `SlimeAddHeat` and `MutateIntoEntity`, and reactions run on the server.
- Wolfgate has no species change effect, so the unstable and imperfect mutation toxins are left out; the green extract
  makes unstable mutagen instead. Advanced mutation toxin (black extract) still turns the drinker into a gray slime,
  and drops what they carried first.
- The fireproof and speed potions work through their own components on the clothing instead of editing its
  protection and speed values. Fireproofing only takes clothing.
- The gold extract's mob tables drop mobs Wolfgate lacks, plus the dungeon dragon, the leviathing, Santa and shadow
  portals.
- Slimes sell for 25 instead of the 1000 every simple mob is worth, so splitting them isn't free money.
