# SuitTank

Spacesuits, hardsuits and modsuit chestplates get an air tank slot. Click the worn (or held) suit with a gas tank to
put it in, and use the suit's Eject verb to take it out. Internals then draw from that tank, so the suit storage slot
stays free for something else. The tank still draws on the wearer, with its suit storage sprite.

Taking the suit off disconnects the tank. Internals pick a tank in this order: back, suit slot, suit storage, then
hands and pockets. Jetpacks don't fit. The tank's own action button and pressure UI only work out of the slot; toggle
internals with the alert or the verb.

Entry points: `WFSuitTankSlotComponent` (put on a suit prototype), `WFSuitTankSlotSystem` (the slot, wearer lookup,
disconnect on unequip), the `SharedInternalsSystem`, `SharedGasTankSystem` and `GasTankSystem` partials that teach
internals about the slot, and `WFSuitTankVisualsSystem` (the mob overlay).
