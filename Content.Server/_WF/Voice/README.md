# Voice

Characters speak their chat aloud as a babble made in code, with no recordings. Each word plays as short
consonant-and-vowel syllables on one pitch, with a closed-mouth hum between words. Commas and full stops pause, a
question lifts the last syllable, an exclamation or an ALL CAPS word comes out louder, an ellipsis trails off and
numbers are said as words. Speech carries 10 tiles and whispers 5, muffled past 2 as in chat, and a listener who
doesn't know the language hears the same scramble chat shows them. A line heard over the radio plays through a
narrow radio sound, a little quieter, in the listener's ear or from the loudspeaker that picked it up. A voice mask
borrows the voice of the character it names, or a voice made from the name.

The creator's Voice tab sets pitch, pitch variance, speed, intonation, vibrato, tremolo and a filter with its
strength, and its Test button plays a typed line. Species filters (Dog, Cat, Bird, Lizard, Mouse, Slime, Plant)
reshape the voice itself, effect filters (Robot, Alien, Insect, Ghost) are laid over it. Options, Audio, Play
character voices turns other players' voices off, and they fall back to their species chatter.

Entry points: `SpeechVoice` (the settings, saved with the profile as JSON in `speech_voice`), the server
`SpeechVoiceSystem` (sets the voice at spawn, profile load and cloning, and sends each listener the text they would
read, for speech and for every radio that receives the line), and
the client `SpeechVoiceSystem` (renders on a worker thread, at most 8 voices at once) and `SpeechVoiceSynth` (builds
the samples). No audio crosses the network. Only characters that join through the normal spawn, and their clones,
get a voice. The mute setting is `SpeechVoiceCVars.Enabled` (`wf.voice.enabled`).
