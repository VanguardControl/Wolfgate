# RolledSleeves

Jumpsuit sleeve rolling, after Meridian Rift's casual jumpsuit style. A player right-clicks a jumpsuit they hold or
wear and picks "Roll up sleeves"; the worn sprite then shows bare forearms. The same verb rolls them back down.

The cut happens at draw time, so every jumpsuit gets it without a drawn rolled sprite. When a rolled suit is
rendered on its wearer, `WFRolledSleevesVisualsSystem` gives its worn layers the `WFRolledSleeves` shader (or
`WFRolledSleevesDisplaced` where the species or body type displaces the suit) and feeds it
`Textures/_WF/RolledSleeves/sleeve_mask.rsi`: alpha 0 removes the sleeve below the elbow, red lightens the cuff row.
`Tools/_WF/RolledSleeves/generate_sleeve_mask.py` builds the mask from the human arm silhouette; `--preview out.png`
draws a before/after sheet. Species-specific worn states and non-32x32 sprites are left unrolled. Suits with short
sleeves just look the same.

Entry points: `WFRolledSleevesAvailabilitySystem` (server; adds `WFRolledSleevesComponent` to all inner clothing at
map init), `WFRolledSleevesSystem` (verb, predicted toggle) and `WFRolledSleevesVisualsSystem` (client; shader and
mask).
