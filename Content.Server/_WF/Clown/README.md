# Clown

The Clown trait's abilities, ported from Wayfarer (`a7f18f6fa85`). A clown juggles two or three held items from an
action button, twists a long balloon into a dog, clown, banana, cat or moth from the right-click menu, and speaks in
Comic Neue. Boxes of long balloons come from the Contractor loadout's Trinkets and the Cuddly Critter vending machine.

Differences from the source: starting to juggle no longer lets go of someone being pulled or a two-handed weapon
being wielded, and items that can't leave the hand stay there. The items arc relative to the camera. The font replaces
any font picked in the profile (`ClownFontSystem`), and radio messages use it too.

Entry points: `JugglingSystem`, `BalloonTwistingSystem`, `ClownFontSystem`, and the client `JugglingOverlay` and `BalloonStringOverlay`.
