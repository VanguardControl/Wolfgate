# Encounters

The Random Encounters System (RES). An encounter is a `wfEncounter` prototype: a set of ships, each with its vessel,
side, crew size, faction, security and disengage rules, and a queue of orders. `WFEncounterSystem` spawns the ships,
crews them and gives them their orders through the `NpcCrew` module, resolves the encounter (expired, orders
completed, one side left, nothing left) and removes its ships once no player is near. `WFEncounterSchedulerSystem`
starts weighted encounters at a random interval in clear space near a player ship; it is off until
`wf.encounters.enabled` is set. Admins use `wf_encounter list | spawn <prototype> [distance] | end <encounter> |
schedule | pause | resume`. Other code calls `WFEncounterSystem.TrySpawn`, `Resolve` and `End` and listens for
`WFEncounterStartedEvent` and `WFEncounterResolvedEvent`.

`Docs/_WF/Encounters/design.md` is the full design. Not built yet: mass scanner contacts and lazy loading, warning
and fire zones, freight, payouts, routes between stations and follow-up encounters.
