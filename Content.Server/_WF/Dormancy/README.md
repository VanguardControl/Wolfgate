# Dormancy

Keeps planets cheap while nobody is on them. Preloaded worlds hold a few hundred thousand entities, and several systems
walked all of them every tick whether a player was near or not. This module cuts that idle cost without changing what
a player standing there sees.

`WFDormancySystem` answers whether a spot is near a player: `IsActive(uid)` is true off-planet, on a planet's orbit
layer, and on any layer of a planet within `Range` (48 m) of a player's body (a player in a vehicle's cabin counts
where the vehicle is). Player positions are bucketed into 16 m chunks per planet, refreshed twice a second and at once
when a player attaches. `IsMapActive(map)` says whether anyone is on that planet at all. Use it to skip per-entity
work on planet ground nobody is near, not to stop timers: a skipped entity should simply pick up again when a player
comes.

What uses it, and the other idle costs fixed here:

- `BodySleeps(uid)`: an NPC body, or an organ in one, on planet ground no player is near skips its upkeep: breath,
  blood, metabolism, digestion and body heat (upstream `RespiratorSystem`, `BloodstreamSystem`, `MetabolizerSystem`,
  `StomachSystem`, `ThermalRegulatorSystem`), and Wolfmed's life tick, wound overlays, synthetic HUD and breathing
  alerts. A body with a mind keeps going wherever it is, so a player's own body, or one they left, never stops.
- `WFReactiveFloraSystem` (LivingWorld): a plant with no player near looks for mobs every 2 s instead of searching
  round itself every half second, and idle plants wait on a timer queue instead of being walked every tick.
- `WFCrewSleepSystem` (NpcCrew) lets a camp or wreck crew on planet ground no player is near sleep, and
  `WFCrewRoutineSystem` turns and patrols only awake crew near a player.
- `WFVehicleCrewSystem` (NpcVehicleCrew): a crewed vehicle with no player near parks where it is, checking once a
  second, instead of driving its patrol unseen, which also kept the pathfinder rebuilding the ground under it.
- Planet water (`FloorWaterEntity`, `MonoFloorWaterEntity`, `WFBloodRiver`, `WFBloodOcean`) carries no
  `SolutionRegeneration`: the upstream one refilled a `tank` solution the water doesn't have, and the blood pools
  hold 9,999,999 units, so it only cost time.
- Wild xeno weeds (`WFXenoWeedsWild`) never spread, so they now inherit `WFXenoWeedsBase`, everything `XenoWeeds`
  has but `XenoWeedsSpreading`, instead of carrying a spreading timer set a billion seconds out.
- `CEZLevelsSystem.WFGroundContact`: a static or sleeping hull's ground contact (the nav screen readout) is sampled
  once every 60 ticks instead of every tick, and the ground friction controller skips sleeping hulls, which have
  nothing to scrape.
- `FlammableSystem.WFSweep`: the once-a-second sweep over every flammable entity runs ten times a second instead of
  every tick.
- `WFVehicleElectricalSystem` (Vehicles) walks the receivers on vehicle cabin maps instead of every power receiver
  in the world.

`PlanetIdleProfileTest` (explicit) builds every round-start world as a round would, with nobody on them, and prints
each system's update cost and what it walks, the physics step's parts and its controllers; run it by hand when tuning.
On a real server, `wf.slow_tick_ms` (0, off, by default) makes `WFTickWatchSystem` log every tick longer than that with
the systems and engine stages that took most of it.
