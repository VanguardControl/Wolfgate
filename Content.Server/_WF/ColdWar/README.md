# ColdWar

The TSF and the PDV start each round under a ceasefire instead of at war. The sector's war level (shown on every PDA as
`WAR LEVEL: COLD` or `HOT`) is what the rules, the lore and the faction guides now hang on: while it is cold the two
factions avoid each other and need a cause to fight, and while it is hot the old rules apply, outpost strikes included.

The Colonel and the Grand Vizier each start with a war declarator. Using it flips their faction's stance. While the
level is cold that is a declaration of war, or its withdrawal; while it is hot it is a ceasefire offer, or its
withdrawal. The level moves when both factions ask for the same change, so one side alone can neither start a war nor
end one. Each stance change goes out on the common channel, a faction waits two minutes between uses, and the standing
level is announced a minute into the round. Admins can still force the level with `setwarlevel`.

Entry points: `ManualPortstrikeRuleSystem.WfUseDeclarator`, a partial hooked into Monolith's declarator handler, and
`WFColdWarComponent` on the `ManualPortstrikeTsfPdv` rule for the cooldown and announcement timings. The rule pages are
Conflict Rule 5 and the Vanguard's standing orders, with marked edits to the Monolith rules, lore and faction pages.
The Encounters module reads the same level: `declaredWar` in `wfStanding` makes TSF and PDV patrols warn each other's
ships off without firing until war is declared, and `requiresWar` keeps the scripted navy battles off the schedule.
