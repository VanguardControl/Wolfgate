# Traders

Dialogue NPCs that front real game systems: a vending machine's stock, shipyard console listings, a used ship lot,
generator refuelling and parcel packing. A customer talks to the trader, picks from the dialogue menu and pays; the
trader opens a shop, shipyard or used ships window, or answers in speech. A trader behind a table can be reached
across it.

Entry points: `TraderSystem` (dialogue, barter zone, payment and receipts), the services `TraderShopSystem`,
`TraderShipyardSystem`, `TraderUsedShipsSystem`, `TraderRefuelSystem` and `TraderParcelSystem`, `TraderComponent` and
`TraderDialoguePrototype`, and the client `TraderBoundUserInterface` with its windows. Traders are defined in
`Resources/Prototypes/_WF/Traders`. `IdCardOwnerSystem` (stamps an ID card with its owner at spawn) and
`InteractionRangeBonusEvent` (reach across the table) exist for traders only.
