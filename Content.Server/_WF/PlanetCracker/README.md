# PlanetCracker

Planets you can land on and capital ships that crack them open. Each world of the sector's star system is a stack of
CE z-levels: an orbit layer, air layers and the ground. Ships hop into orbit from the sector, descend through the
atmosphere on landing thrusters and land, or fall and crash when lift is lost. A cracker ship anchors over a world,
cuts a chunk out of it and hauls the chunk up for mining; the crack scars the surface and can unearth deep veins,
fissures and the carcinoma infestation.

Players use the shuttle console's orbit button and ascend/descend controls, the survey and crack consoles, the
centrifuge and gravity anchors, landing thruster kits, parachutes and the planet timepiece. Worlds have day and night,
phased weather with thunder, fauna, ambience, a gravity well that pulls adrift hulls into orbit, and an approach
cinematic on the hop in. Admins get the Planet Control panel in the Wolfgate admin tab (`planetcontrol`) for sanction,
time of day and weather, `wfplanet` to build a star system's worlds, and `wfcracker` for testing.

Entry points: `WFPlanetNetworkSystem` and `WFPlanetRegistrySystem` (building the z-level stack per world),
`WFOrbitEntrySystem` (hops in and out of orbit), `WFFlightSystem` (atmospheric flight, liftoff, landing and crashes),
`WFCrackerSystem` with `WFCrackConsoleSystem` and `WFPlanetChunkSystem` (the crack), `WFSurveyConsoleSystem`,
`WFPlanetWeatherSystem`, `WFGravityWellSystem`, `WFParachuteSystem` and `PlanetControlSystem`. Upstream systems are
extended through partials in this folder (`CEZLevelsSystem.WF*`, `ThrusterSystem.WF*`, `ShuttleSystem.WFOrbit`).
Settings are in `PlanetCrackerCVars`; design notes and plans are in `Docs/_WF/PlanetCracker`.
