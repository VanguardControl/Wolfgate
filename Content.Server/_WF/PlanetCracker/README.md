# PlanetCracker

Capital ships that crack planets open; builds on the Planets module. A cracker parks in a world's orbit, its crew
ferries two gravity anchors down in crates and drills them in, and the crack console cuts a disc out of the ground
between them with the gravity projectors, then hauls it up into the hull's berth where crack miners strip it. The cut
scars the surface, surveys find deep veins, fissures spawn site threats around the anchors, and an unsanctioned crack
draws sector notices. Players use the survey console, the handheld surveyor, the crack console, the centrifuge and
the anchors; admins use `wfcracker` (test hulls and state shortcuts) and `wfsurvey`.

Entry points: `WFCrackerSystem` with `WFCrackConsoleSystem` and `WFPlanetChunkSystem` (the crack), `WFGravityAnchorSystem`,
`WFSurveyConsoleSystem`, `WFDeepVeinSystem`, `WFFissureSpawnerSystem`, `WFCrackMinerSystem` and `WFCrackSanctionSystem`.
Per-world data (vein table, fissure factions, deep-vein marker layers) is in `wfCrackSite` prototypes. It plugs into
Planets through `WFDetachedTerrainComponent`, `WFLiftoffAttemptEvent`, `WFGridLiftLoadEvent`,
`WFPlanetGroundSpawnedEvent` and `WFOrbitLayerComponent.RadarScars`. Settings are in `PlanetCrackerCVars`
(`wf.planet_cracker.announce`); design notes, plans and the playtest checklist are in `Docs/_WF/PlanetCracker`.

It also edits two other Wolfgate modules without markers: `AdminVesselSpawnSystem` binds the anchors of an admin- or
ERT-spawned cracker, and `WolfgateAdminCommands` holds the `wfcracker` and `wfsurvey` names.
