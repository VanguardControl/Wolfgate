# AppearanceStudio

A layer editor for characters, opened with the Studio button under the creator's preview. The window shows the
character on a big doll with an outliner of its body parts, markings, worn items and held items (the doll holds a
sample item picked from the toolbar). The selected part can be moved a pixel at a time, by dragging on the doll or
with the inspector's buttons, with a separate position for each facing. It can also be scaled, hidden in game (body
parts and markings only) or warped (worn and held items only). A timeline plays, pauses, steps and speeds up the
doll's animated layers, all of them or one part's, and the outliner's checkboxes hide parts in the preview. Playback
and preview visibility are not saved.

A warp is a displacement map the player paints in the warp editor: push the item's pixels into shape over the body,
smooth, restore or cut them, per facing, mirrored or copied across. It replaces the species map for that slot or hand.
A warp can only bend an item inside its own 32x32 frame; Position moves it further.

Everything is saved in the profile as tweaks (`StudioTweak`: target, key, four offsets, scale, hidden, warp hash),
stored as text in `profile.appearance_studio` and copied onto the body's `HumanoidAppearanceComponent`, so other
players see it.

## How it fits together

- `StudioSpriteSystem` (client) applies tweaks every frame for the facing the body shows: world rotation plus eye
  rotation, a sprite's direction override, or the facing a preview set with `SetPreviewFacing`. It remembers what
  it added to each layer, so other systems' offsets stay, a rebuilt layer starts clean, and removing a tweak puts
  the layer back. A body tweak also moves the markings on that part.
- Warps are stored as one-frame custom marking art (`StudioWarp`: red 128 + x step, green 128 - y step, alpha 255
  draw / 0 cut). They share CustomMarkings' art table, fetching, daily limit, unused-art cleanup and admin blocking.
  The server checks every pixel draws from inside its frame, since a step outside would sample a neighbouring sprite
  in the atlas. The client loads a warp as a second, linear (non-sRGB) RSI, `warp-<hash>`, and
  `ClientClothingSystem` and `HandsSystem` ask `StudioSpriteSystem` for it before the species map.
- Limits are cvars: `wf.appearance_studio.enabled`, `max_offset` (16 px, hard cap 48), `min_scale` / `max_scale`
  (0.5 to 2) and `warps`. The profile and the body are both cleaned to them.

## Moderation

The `WFAppearanceStudio` feature ban (FeatureBans module) stops a player saving warps or custom markings, and
removes their studio tweaks and custom markings from their body when the ban lands and whenever they spawn. Admins
also have a Reset Appearance Studio verb on any tweaked body, and `custommarkingblock <hash>` blocks a warp's art,
which removes it from every body.
