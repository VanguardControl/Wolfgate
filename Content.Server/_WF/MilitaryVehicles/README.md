# MilitaryVehicles

Military vehicles for players: bare chassis, hardpoints sold separately, and the heavy vehicle licence that gates them.
Built on the VehicleShop terminals and the Vehicles hardpoint framework.

- **Military outfitter** (`WFComputerMilitaryOutfitter`): sells bare chassis onto its pad (`WFMilitaryCatalogChassis`:
  humvee, APC, PMC APC, medical APC, AEV, FV150 light tank, tank, Longstreet; wheels or treads and basic armour, turret
  and weapon mounts empty) and hands parts over the counter (`WFMilitaryCatalogParts`: turrets, guns, armour, treads,
  wheels, utility parts and ammunition crates). No map carries one yet.
- **Station counter** (`WFComputerMilitaryCounter`, beside the ATMs on Caelestinus Central): sells the licence
  (`WFMilitaryCatalogCounter`, 25,000) and the same parts. Drop beacons for the chassis go in a catalogue of their own,
  added to its `extraCatalogs`.
- **Heavy vehicle licence** (`WFHeavyVehicleLicence`, `WFHeavyVehicleLicenceSystem`): made out to its buyer by name.
  Listings marked `licence: true` (every chassis, turret and gun) are refused to anyone not carrying one. The jobs in
  `wfLicenceIssue` prototypes (NFSD, TSF and PDV fighting and command jobs) spawn with one in the bag, made out to them.
- **Weapons on military chassis only** (`WFMilitaryHardpointSystem`): a gun, a turret, or a part carrying a gun can't be
  fitted to a vehicle without `WFMilitaryVehicle` (tanks, APCs, the armed humvees, the armed SPP van, the AEV and the
  chassis here). It hooks `HardpointFitAttemptEvent`, raised when someone starts fitting a part.

Every listing sells above what the item appraises for, fitted parts included (`MilitaryVehiclesTest`); sold parts carry
a `StaticPrice` of about 70% of their listing.
