# MilitaryVehicles

Military ground vehicles for players: chassis, parts, the heavy vehicle licence, orbital drops and capturing NPC crews'
vehicles.

## Orbital drops, capture and salvage

Orbital drop beacons (`WFOrbitalDropSystem`, `Resources/Prototypes/_WF/MilitaryVehicles/orbital_drop.yml`) call a chassis
down from orbit. Used in hand on a planet's ground layer, under open sky, the beacon is set down and twenty seconds later
the vehicle lands on it, square to the ground, with a rumble six seconds out, a heavy landing and dust; its key goes to
whoever set it off. Off a planet's ground (orbit, air, caverns, a ship, station or building grid), under a roof or an
upper level, or with something solid in the vehicle's room, it says why: in hand before it is set down, or at the drop,
when it stops and can be set off again. `WFMilitaryCatalogDrops` lists the beacons for the station counter (APC 65,000,
AEV 100,000, tank 140,000) with the military repair kit (9,000); an unused beacon resells for 75% of the lower of the
vehicle's value and its listing.

The military vehicle repair kit (`WFVehicleCaptureSystem`, `capture.yml`) makes a vehicle of any size whole, once. Used
on an NPC crew's vehicle with no living crewman aboard (all dead, or bailed out), it also claims it: the NPC brain and
faction go, the lock gets a new key id, and the user is handed the only key.

Salvage (`WFVehicleSalvageSystem`): an NPC crew's vehicle, a claimed one (`WFSalvagedComponent`) and any part taken off
either, or off a part still on one, appraise for 25% of their new value wherever they are sold, so a delivery pad pays
75% of that. A part fitted to a claimed vehicle afterwards keeps its own value.
