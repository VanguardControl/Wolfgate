# GroundCover

Weather that stays on the ground. Rain wets open ground: it darkens with a faint sheen and puddles spread in fixed
noise spots, growing with the rain and drying over minutes after it stops. Snow weather lays snow cover on open tiles,
up to three levels deep; over one level it slows walking, a shovel or spade clears a tile, and it melts once no snow
falls and the tile's air is above 0 C. Roofed and indoor tiles stay dry and clear, by WolfWeather's own cover rules.

How wet and snowy a weather makes the ground is set on each `wolfWeather` with `groundWetness` (0 to 1; puddles above
0.3) and `snowfall` (cover levels a minute). Wetness is one value per map (`WFGroundWeatherComponent`), networked in
fiftieths. Snow is a byte per tile in 16-tile chunks (`WFSnowCoverComponent` on the grid, server only), kept only where
snow has fallen. Chunks are worked a few a tick and only near players; one nobody saw catches up from the map's running
totals of snowfall and snow-free time when someone comes near. Each player is sent the chunks around them that changed
since they were last sent (`WFSnowCoverChunksMessage`). The client draws both in one shader over the floor, under
everything else; the Weather detail option turns the wet ground off (Off) or leaves out puddles (Reduced). Snow always
draws, as it slows walking.

Entry points: `WFGroundCoverSystem.StepMap`, `UpdateChunk`, `GetDepth`, `SetDepth`, `GetTileWetness` (server), the
client `WFGroundCoverOverlay`.
