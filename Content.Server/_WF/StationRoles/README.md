# StationRoles

Station Roles is the Join Round option for taking a job on a station; it replaced the old Join Game window's two tabs.
Ship crews join through Crew Setup only, so the window is now only the station list, and paid jobs show their wage.
The old route onto ships is gone too: ship records consoles no longer open job slots or take adverts, and ordinary
joining refuses player ships.

Station roles are paid automatically. Each `wfStationSalary` prototype (`Resources/Prototypes/_WF/StationRoles`) names
a job, its hourly wage and the sector account that pays it; jobs without one earn nothing. `WFStationSalarySystem` puts
a `WFStationRoleComponent` on a body spawned into a salaried job on a station, counts a minute of duty while its player
is in that body, alive or critical, not AFK and not in a crew, and pays every 15 duty minutes. A short account pays
everyone the same share. A job with no account draws nothing.
