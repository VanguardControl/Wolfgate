# PoiArrivals

Some points of interest aren't in the sector when the shift starts and jump in later, announced to everyone. The
Anomalous Lab and Sevastopol each arrive at their own random time between 15 and 45 minutes into the shift.

A `wgPoiArrival` prototype (`Content.Shared`, so the client knows the kind) names a `pointOfInterest` in `poi` and
sets its delay range, announcement, sender and colour. `WGPoiArrivalSystem.Defer` is called from the adventure rule's
round-start sort with the sector map and holds such a POI back instead of spawning it. When its time comes, `WarpIn`
sets it up as its station on a holding map through `PointOfInterestSystem.WgSpawnArrival` (the same setup as a
round-start POI, at a spot clear of the stations already placed), takes the holding map off the FTL destination list,
then FTLs the grid to a clear spot beside that point. On arrival it deletes the holding map, gives a station with dead
drops its own roll of them (the sector's were shared out at round start) and announces the range and bearing from the
sector core. A grid that can't jump is set down in place and announced at once. Nothing arrives or is announced once
the round has left `InRound`.

Admins use `wg_poiarrivals` (`WGPoiArrivalCommand`): with no argument it lists the stations still to come and when,
and `wg_poiarrivals <poi id>` or `wg_poiarrivals all` jumps them in now, ahead of schedule.

Contractors find their stations by name each time the board posts, skipping a station whose grid is still jumping in,
so an arriving station's contractor posts nothing until it is there and starts once it arrives.

Sevastopol's vault used to sit behind a 30-minute timed seal (`WindowTimedSeal`); with the station arriving mid-shift
the seal is removed from its map.
