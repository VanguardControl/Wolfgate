# PoiArrivals

Some points of interest aren't in the sector when the shift starts and jump in later, announced to everyone. The
Anomalous Lab and Sevastopol each arrive at their own random time between 30 and 90 minutes into the shift.

A `wgPoiArrival` prototype, with the same ID as the `pointOfInterest`, marks a POI as arriving and sets its delay
range, announcement, sender and colour. `WGPoiArrivalSystem.Defer` is called from the adventure rule's round-start
sort and holds such a POI back instead of spawning it. When its time comes, `WarpIn` sets it up as its station on a
holding map through `PointOfInterestSystem.WgSpawnArrival` (the same setup as a round-start POI, at a spot clear of
the stations already placed), then FTLs it to a clear spot beside that point. On arrival it deletes the holding map,
gives a station with dead drops its own roll of them (the sector's were shared out at round start) and announces the
range and bearing from the sector core. A grid that can't jump is set down in place and announced at once.

Admins use `wg_poiarrivals` (`WGPoiArrivalCommand`): with no argument it lists the stations still to come and when,
and `wg_poiarrivals <poi id>` or `wg_poiarrivals all` jumps them in now, ahead of schedule.

Contractors find their stations by name each time the board posts, so an arriving station's contractor posts nothing
until it is there and starts once it arrives.
