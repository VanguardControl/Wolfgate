# MentorObserve

Mentors going to the player they are helping, from a Mentor Help ticket they hold and only after the player allows
it (feature plan 4.5, D10, D11). `WGMentorVisitSystem` runs both kinds of visit: watching, here, and joining as a
critter (the body is the [MentorCritters module](../MentorCritters/README.md)).

- **Asking:** Watch or Join as a critter on the selected ticket in the mentor window. The asker gets a prompt in
  their Mentor Help window (Allow / Not now) and can stop it there at any time, or take a riding critter out.
- **A ghost mentor** follows the asker and sees through the asker's eye with field of view until they move away,
  press Stop following, or the visit ends. Trainees may only do this (D4).
- **A living mentor steps out** (Mentor tier and up): their mind visits a `WGMentorObserver`, an eye that follows the
  asker and is not a ghost, so it can't warp or follow anyone else. The body stays, marked "They're away helping
  someone." (`WGMentorAwayComponent`). Refused while hurt in the last minute, critical, dead or cuffed, while holding
  an antagonist role, or within 30 seconds of the last step-out.
- **Back:** Return to body (an action, Shift+F3 `WGMentorReturn`, or the button), and on its own when
  `wg.mentor.step_out_minutes` (10) runs out, the body takes damage or falls, the ticket closes, or the asker stops it.
- Staff get an admin alert when a visit starts and ends; both go to the admin log (`WGMentor`) and the ticket's
  stored rows (roles `watch` and `critter`), which the leaderboard counts.

The SS13 keybind preset (`Content.Client/_WG/Options/Ss13KeybindPreset.cs`) keeps ShowDebugMonitors on Shift+F3 and
puts Return to body on Ctrl+F3.
