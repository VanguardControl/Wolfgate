# MentorCritters

Mentor critters (feature plan 4.6, D7 to D9, D16): a Mentor in the round beside the player they are helping, as one of
seven animals (`wgMentorCritter` prototypes: mouse, hamster, mothroach, frog, lizard, butterfly, snail), for
Mentor tier and up while `wg.mentor.critters` is on. The visit itself (asking, the mind going in and out, ending) is
`WGMentorVisitSystem` in [MentorObserve](../MentorObserve/README.md); `WGMentorCritterSystem` is the body.

- **Body** (`WGMentorCritterBase`): no damage at all, isn't food, can't be butchered, picked up, pulled or thrown,
  isn't a ghost role, pacified. One hand for tiny and small things and simple use; machine and console windows are
  refused (D9). Drawn amber with a soft halo (`WGMentorCritterVisualsSystem`) and named "Mentor <animal>".
- **Riding** (D8): Climb into pocket (tiny and small critters, an empty pocket of a worn jumpsuit) and Climb onto head
  (the mouse and the mothroach, which have a worn look), after a short climb, only on the asker and only if their
  consent toggle "Mentor critters may ride on me" (`WGMentorCritterRide`, on by default) allows it. Nobody else can
  take it out; the asker can, from their Mentor Help window. From a pocket it can only talk and point.
- **Talking** (D7): plain speech, no language or accent. By default only the asker hears it (and ghosts, which hear
  direct speech, so watching Mentors and Staff do); Speak aloud switches to local chat.
- **Point it out:** a marker only the asker sees, for a few seconds.
- Brought back to the asker when it ends up in a container, in space, on another grid or far away.
