# StockExchange

Grid exchanges with real order books for materials. A market lives on a grid (`WGStockMarketComponent`) and is reached
through `WGStockExchangeConsole`; its goods come from a `wgStockMarket` prototype. Players place limit bids and asks,
buy from the cheapest asks or sell to the highest bids, and cancel their own resting orders. The book matches in price
then time order, holding buy money and sell goods until an order trades or is cancelled.

Every character trades for one account: their gameplay faction when their job assigns one, otherwise their player
company. That is also the precedence rule: a member of both acts for the faction, and their company account is not
reachable from the terminal. `WGStockAccountComponent`, one hidden entity per faction in the round or per company,
holds the account's open listings and its material stock. Market terminal modes are:

- `WGStockExchangeConsole`: the order book, account funds and holdings.
- `WGStockMaterialSilo`: round materials and, for companies, the persistent silo in separate panels.

Bought materials go straight into the buyer's account storage, never onto a map. A company fills its purchased
persistent silo first and puts the rest in round storage; a faction only has round storage. Materials are counted in
sheets, with one sheet equal to 100 material units. Each completed trade messages the affected faction or company.

Quick buy, quick sell and the sell pad's Sell all work for anyone. Only a faction or company account may place a
resting limit order. A character with no account pays from cash they carry (thalers in hands, inventory or containers) and, when
buying, receives the material on the exchange console's attached `WGStockDeliveryPad` instead of account storage. Cash
is always spent before the character's faction or company funds. A character with no account who sells is handed the
proceeds as cash immediately. The delivery pad is linked with the same mapping device linker
(`DeviceLinkSource` port `WGStockExchangeConsole` to `DeviceLinkSink` port `WGStockDeliveryPad`), or any delivery pad on
the console's grid is used.

The delivery pad is only for quick buys by characters with no faction or company. `WGStockSellSpot` is a passive
pad. A `WGStockSellConsole` is linked to it with the mapping tools' device linker
(`DeviceLinkSource` port `WGStockSellConsole` to `DeviceLinkSink` port `WGStockSellPad`), and reads every material on
the pad and inside any container placed on it. Its window lists each buyer's share, a break row after each line and the
total, and its Sell all button sells only the materials that have buy orders. The crate and every non-material or
unwanted good stay where they are.

A faction's persistent silo does not exist: its materials last for the round. A company opens with zero persistent
space and buys it a sheet at a time for 1,000,000 thalers from its bank (`WGCompanySystem.StockBuySiloSlots`). Persistent
silo stock is written through to `WolfgateCompany.SiloSlots` and `WolfgateCompany.SiloMaterials` through
`SetCompanySiloAsync`, so it survives round restarts. Withdrawing from the persistent silo needs the new
`WGCompanyPermissions.SiloWithdraw` (the owner has it, and new Officer ranks get it). Depositing into the persistent silo
has no separate permission.

The PDA app `WGStockRemoteCartridge` (`WGStockRemoteUi`) shows every open market's quotes and the viewer's own stores,
but has no actions. It is installed on every PDA like the Market app.

Entry points: `WGStockSystem` (accounts, matching, terminals, trade messages), `WGStockSystem.Cash` (cash-first
payment and the delivery pad), `WGStockSystem.Storage` (stock, silo, spawning), `WGStockSystem.Ui` (the market window's
state and actions), `WGStockSystem.SellConsole` (the linked sell pad's receipt and Sell all),
`WGStockSystem.Interaction` (silo deposits), `WGStockSystem.Remote` (the read-only PDA app),
`WGCompanySystem.StockExchange` (company bank, permissions, silo persistence and member notices), `WGStockWindow`,
`WGStockSellConsoleWindow` and `WGStockRemoteUi`.
