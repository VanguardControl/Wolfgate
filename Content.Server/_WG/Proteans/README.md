# Proteans

Proteans are a nanite-slime species: a swarm of machines around one solid seed. This module is the body so far
(PR B1 of the Protean workplan); collapse and reform, the harness and plates, being worn and the puddle and mimic
forms come in later PRs. The species (`WGProtean`) is not round-start yet, so only admins can spawn one
(`MobWGProtean`).

What a Protean is now:

- Built on `BaseMobSpecies`, not the organic base, so it has no blood, breathing, hunger, thirst, wounds or pain.
  Slime sprites, markings and voice for now. Its body has the usual part layout with one organ, the seed
  (`WGProteanSeed`), and no part can be severed or destroyed.
- Fragile: damage set `WGProtean` (Blunt 1.2, Slash and Piercing 1.3, Heat 1.2, Shock 1.5; Poison, Radiation,
  Cellular, Asphyxiation and Bloodloss do nothing). Critical at 75, dead at 150 until collapse replaces both.
- Synthetic and locked there: `biotypeLocked` on the species makes `WGBiotypeRules.Resolve` ignore a stored Organic,
  and the creator greys out the other button.
- Nanomass is the charge of the seed's battery (0 to 100). `WGNanomassSystem` spends it on upkeep (a full load
  lasts about 33 minutes, 16 times longer as a puddle) and healing (above 30 mass, 30 s after the last hit),
  sheds it as `WGProteanSlurry` when hurt (0.3 a point, walked back up by any Protean), burns it on an EMP
  (10 heavy, 5 light, and a knockdown; the seed itself ignores EMPs) and slows the Protean below 5.
- It eats: the Absorb verb on anything whose materials are in the `WGProtean` diet (`nanomass.yml`), as much of a
  stack as fits (ten steel sheets fill an empty body); someone else can Feed it the item in their hand, which also
  heals 20. Living things show the verb too, so the refusal can be read. It refuses the living, containers holding things, paid
  augments and keys, IDs and money. Plasteel, glass, silver or gold, plasma and uranium leave a ten-minute trace
  status effect (`WGProteanTraceComponent`): Hardened takes 0.8 damage, Surging heals three times as fast and
  takes 1.5 Heat, Self-sustaining halves upkeep and glows; Limber and Conductive wait for forms and modules.
- No augments: `WGNoAugmentsComponent` (Augments module) closes every slot in the creator, shows why on the
  Augments tab and makes autosurgeons refuse. No surgery: `WGProteanSystem` strips `SurgeryTargetComponent`, which
  every surgery and the autodoc need, and answers a surgical tool with `wg-protean-surgery-refuse`.
- No hardsuits, as a joke: anything tagged `Hardsuit`, or attached to such a suit, is refused on
  `IsEquippingAttemptEvent`, whoever is dressing the Protean. The line shows above the Protean, not at the cursor:
  `InventorySystem.WgEquipRefusalOnMob` (marked calls in `InventorySystem.Equip.cs` and the strip menu). Forced equips (job loadouts) skip that event; the
  harness PR handles loadouts.

Entry points: `WGProteanSystem` (shared: surgery, hardsuits, examine, seed EMP, thin slowdown) on
`WGProteanComponent`; `WGNanomassSystem` (server: everything that moves mass; `GetMass`, `SetMass`, `TryAbsorb`,
`SpillSlurry`, `Reclaim`) on `WGNanomassComponent`; `WGProteanDietPrototype` (`wgProteanDiet`) and
`WGProteanTraceComponent` for what materials give. Prototypes: `species.yml` (species, speech verb, damage set),
`body.yml` (body, parts, seed), `mobs.yml` (mob and creator dummy), `nanomass.yml` (diet, slurry, traces, alerts).
Every tuning number is a DataField on those components or prototypes. Strings are in
`Resources/Locale/en-US/_WG/Proteans/proteans.ftl`; lines Moon hasn't seen sit under its `## Draft` heading.

Absorbing and feeding go in the admin log as `WGProtean`. Tests: `ProteanBodyTest` (body, damage, biotype, augments,
hardsuits) and `ProteanNanomassTest` (upkeep, absorbing and refusals, feeding, slurry, traces, EMP).

A new species makes `WolfmedRangeMapTest` fail until the range map is regenerated (`WolfmedRangeMapGenerator`,
explicit, with `WOLFMED_RANGE_OUT` set).
