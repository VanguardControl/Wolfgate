# LogInvestigator

The admin log window, rebuilt for investigations (`wg.admin_logs.investigator`, on by default; `adminlogs`, the
entity Logs verb and the Player Panel's Logs button open it).

- **Query:** a round and a round-time window, players (any of them, or only logs involving all of them), entities,
  categories of log types (`Resources/Prototypes/_WG/LogInvestigator/log_categories.yml`; every type is in one),
  impact, and text in the message. Text and ids are matched as substrings, so `18204606` and partial words are found
  in every round. "Only players near the picked log" narrows it to the players who came within the radius of the
  selected log's position. A time it can't read is reported instead of ignored. Results page by log id, newest or
  oldest first; changing the round clears them and searches the new one.
- **Results:** each log wraps to the list's width, its type coloured by impact. Bursts of the same event between the
  same entities fold into one row ("×14 over 6 s"); click to unfold. Picking a log lists the entities it names with
  Filter, Follow and VV (current round only, since entity ids are reused), Nearby and Timeline. The first row of an
  unfolded run folds it again. On the current round a newest-first list with no end time stays live (Live,
  on by default): newer logs arrive every 5 s, at the top, or behind a "new logs" button while the list is scrolled down, hovered or
  has a log picked. Opened
  from an open ticket, the window has no end time for the same reason.
- **Reports** (deterministic, from the logs alone): Encounter (first strike, who hit whom with hits, damage and
  weapons, turret hits credited to whoever fired the ship's guns, deaths, what the parties said and hailed in the
  10 minutes before, and who was nearby); Timeline (each chosen player's round in phases, up to 5 players, each
  phase's logs drawn like the result list); Nearby (who came within the radius of a log's position, on the same grid, alive, and whether they
  spoke), with a button that filters the results to those players and the minute around it. The radius box sets
  Nearby's, Encounter's and the near filter's radius; it is a strict distance, and sharing a grid is noted but doesn't
  count. A report that hit its 5000-row cap for a kind of log says so at the top.
- **Positions:** the log types in `wg.admin_logs.position_types` get each entity's map, grid and position under
  `wgPos` in their JSON, and `WGPlayerPositionSystem` stores every player's position every
  `wg.admin_logs.position_interval` seconds (10) in `wolfgate_player_position`. Prune that table with `admin_log`
  (WUFF's `prune-admin-logs.ps1`).
- Commands: `wglogs [round N] [player ID] [entity ID] [text ...]`, and `wginvestigate KEY` for an ahelp ticket.

This module also carries the upstream log window's fixes: substring search on Postgres instead of English full text
search (which never found ids), cursor paging in the cache, the requested order and impacts applied on the server,
"all players" meaning every one of them, type tags on log lines, and non-player logs shown by default.
