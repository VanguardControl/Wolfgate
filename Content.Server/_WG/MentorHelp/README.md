# MentorHelp

Mentor Help: a ticket a player opens for a "how do I...?" question, answered by mentors on duty. A player has one
open ticket at a time; mentors claim, hand on, escalate to staff and resolve it, and the player rates it after. An
unclaimed ticket falls back to staff as a Gameplay ahelp. Players reach it from the menu bar, the lobby, Shift+F1,
`mhelp` and the how-to topic in Admin Help; staff can send an ahelp over to it.

`WGMentorHelpSystem` holds the round's tickets in memory and writes each message and state change to the database in
the background (`MentorTicket.Writes`). Responders are on-duty mentors (from `WGMentorSystem` in the `Mentors`
module) and staff with Adminhelp or Event. A ticket marked as about a secret role reaches only mentors out of the
round, and staff. Names follow decision D6: the asker sees responders only as "Mentor", responders see the asker only
by character name (the one played, or the one selected in the lobby), and staff see both. A responder's first post
claims an unclaimed ticket. The fallback puts the player's question into their ahelp as a Gameplay issue through
`BwoinkSystem.SendFromMentorHelp` (a partial here). Players can also use `mhelp <question>`.

On the client, `WGClientMentorHelpSystem` holds what the server sends, and `MentorHelpUIController` opens
`MentorHelpWindow` from the menu bar button, the lobby menu and Shift+F1 (`WGMentorHelpKeys`). The window has two tabs:
the player's own question (ask with a topic and the secret-role box, reply, end, rate, reopen) and, for responders,
the queue. In the queue, mentors claim, unclaim, take over with a note, hand a question to another mentor, resolve
and reply, and switch their duty. The menu button turns red when a reply or a new question arrives while the window
is closed.

## Data

- `wolfgate_mentor_ticket`: one row per ticket, with the character name mentors saw, topic, flags, who held the claim
  (credited with the resolve), how it closed (`resolved`, `player`, `idle`, `round_end`, `escalated`, `fallback`)
  and the rating (1, -1 or none).
- `wolfgate_mentor_ticket_message`: each line, with its sender and role (`player`, `mentor`, `staff`, `system`).
  After `wg.mentor.transcript_days` the text and the rating comment are cleared; the rows stay for the numbers.
- `wolfgate_mentor_canned`: canned answers, each with an optional guidebook entry.

Read and written through `ServerDbManager.MentorHelp.cs`.
