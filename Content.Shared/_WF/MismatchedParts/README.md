# MismatchedParts

The Mismatched parts option, as on Nova Sector: a per-character checkbox in the creator's Body card, saved with the
profile. With it on, the character may wear every species' markings, hair and facial hair. The species' point budgets
still apply, and a category the species has no points for gets one. Body parts only some species' sprites have
(Resomi tail extras, harpy arm extensions) stay with those species. Random profiles leave it off, and turning it off
in the creator drops whatever only the option allowed.

`MismatchedPartsRules` holds the rules. `MarkingSet.ForProfile` and `OpenPoints` open the budgets, and the
`EnsureSpecies` overload keeps other species' markings; validation, the body (`LoadProfile`), the creator doll and the
creator's marking picker all go through them. Hair and facial hair are saved outside the marking set, so a style the
species can't wear on its own is added as a forced marking after the normal hair path.
`HumanoidAppearanceComponent.MismatchedParts` lets the client draw and colour markings without a species layer.

A species wears a hair category on its own when its marking points allow it, its sprite set has a layer for it that
takes markings, and it has at least one style; without the option, a species that can't is offered none.

The barber mirror doesn't know about the option yet, so it only offers the species' own hair.

`MismatchedPartsTest` covers the rules, validation, the saved column, random profiles, and the hair, facial hair and
another species' marking on the bodies and creator dolls of every playable species; `MismatchedPartsBodyTest` a
networked body and its clone.
