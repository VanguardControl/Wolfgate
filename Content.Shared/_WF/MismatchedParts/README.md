# MismatchedParts

The Mismatched parts option: a per-character checkbox in the creator's Body card, saved with the profile. For now it
lets a species that can't wear hair or facial hair pick from the shared styles (every style no species restricts, plus
the species' own). A species that already wears a category keeps its own styles either way. Random profiles leave it
off. It is meant to grow into the gate for other cross-species markings.

A species wears a category on its own when its marking points allow it, its sprite set has a layer for it that takes
markings, and it has at least one style (`MismatchedPartsRules`). With the option on, the body gets the style as a
forced marking after the species filter, and `HumanoidAppearanceComponent.MismatchedParts` lets the client draw and
colour it without a species layer. The creator doll does the same client-side.

The barber mirror doesn't know about the option yet, so it only offers the species' own styles.

`MismatchedPartsTest` covers the rules, validation, the saved column, random profiles, and the bodies and creator dolls
of every playable species; `MismatchedPartsBodyTest` a networked body and its clone.
