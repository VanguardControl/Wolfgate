# ClownCosmetics

Clown and mime cosmetics ported from other forks: 33 facepaint markings and 35 clothing items. The facepaints are
under Head markings, and the clothing is in the 'Contractor' loadout which multiple roles inherit.

The facepaints are from [Frontier #3841](https://github.com/new-frontiers-14/frontier-station-14/pull/3841) by
Chedd-Error, with sprites by Zergologist (CC-BY-SA-4.0). The clothing is from Impstation, Starlight, Space Station 14
and Coyote Sector, all CC-BY-SA-3.0; each RSI's `meta.json` keeps its source's credit. The mime boots' footsteps are
Impstation's `FootstepBoots` (CC0-1.0), credited in `attributions.yml`.

Differences from the sources: the facepaints have no species restriction, and the reptilian serious mime facepaint,
which Frontier never defined, is added. Sprite states for species Wolfgate doesn't have are left out. The clown nose
has no breath mask, the Impstation clown shoes have no waddle toggle, the security mime mask isn't contraband, and
the Coyote outfits sit on `ClothingUniformSkirtBase`.
