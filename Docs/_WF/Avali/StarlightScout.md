# Starlight Avali scout

Compared Wolfgate with Starlight `starlight-dev` at `7c5b54d32cc52526f66b3d79ec3fea6926ab7108` (2026-09-29). This is a bounded review of Avali-specific landed changes, not a full fork diff.

## Port candidates

1. **Bound the nanite shell — highest gameplay priority.** [Starlight PR #5142](https://github.com/ss14Starlight/space-station-14/pull/5142), merged as [`2a67e1a`](https://github.com/ss14Starlight/space-station-14/commit/2a67e1af2d511090f2e1d839307fa4b5169a56cc), tracks positive incoming damage before shell reduction and exits stasis at 50 damage. It also counts actual healing and exits after 50 damage healed, resetting both counters at entry and exit. The Avali prototype sets `stasisHealth` and `healingThreshold` to 50. Wolfgate’s [StasisSystem](../../../Content.Server/_Starlight/Actions/Stasis/StasisSystem.cs) has critical-state exits and per-state healing/resistance, but no damage or healing totals and no equivalent cap. Its [StasisComponent](../../../Content.Shared/_Starlight/Actions/Stasis/StasisComponent.cs) currently defaults additional damage resistance to `1.5`, while its comment describes a 0–1 range. Port the finite-shell behavior as a system change after reviewing these differing rules; adding only the two YAML fields would do nothing.

2. **Add missing Avali gasmask sprites.** [Starlight PR #5362](https://github.com/ss14Starlight/space-station-14/pull/5362) adds `equipped-MASK-avali` states to the regular gas mask, security gas mask, and Starlight corpsman mask. In Wolfgate, `Resources/Textures/Clothing/Mask/gas.rsi` and `gassecurity.rsi` have metadata but no Avali equipped-state PNG; the `_Starlight` corpsman mask is also missing its Avali PNG. The PR adds the corresponding art and registers each state in `meta.json`. This is a contained visual port.

3. **Remove the custom hunger thresholds and correct the label.** [Starlight PR #5992](https://github.com/ss14Starlight/space-station-14/pull/5992) keeps Avali `baseDecayRate: 0.03`, identifies it as 1.8× the default rate, removes the custom threshold overrides, and changes the guidebook rate from 2× to 1.8×. Wolfgate’s [Avali prototype](../../../Resources/Prototypes/_StarLight/Entities/Mobs/Species/avali.yml) still says “on 2x more” and sets thresholds to 250/200/150/100/0. The PR says the overrides differ from defaults only around recovering from zero hunger; check the intended recovery behavior before removing them.

## Already covered or not ready to port

- **Core species behavior is present:** the Avali body and organ prototypes, own blood, ammonia interaction, cold-temperature tuning, temperature speed penalties, low-gravity movement, species voice/emotes, and Avali markings are in Wolfgate. [Starlight PR #4386](https://github.com/ss14Starlight/space-station-14/pull/4386) fixed Avali body-part blood and ammonia-gas handling; Wolfgate’s corresponding body/chemistry port already has Avali blood and ammonia rules. The proposed Dexalin/saline change remains an [open Starlight issue #5148](https://github.com/ss14Starlight/space-station-14/issues/5148), not a landed patch, and conflicts with Wolfgate’s current HardLight chemistry behavior.
- **Large-caliber knockback removal is already reflected in local weapon prototypes.** PR #5142 removes Avali-specific knockback/stamina entries from Starlight weapon files. Wolfgate’s corresponding HMG, LMG, launcher, rifle, shotgun, and sniper prototypes contain no Avali-specific entries.
- **Stasis animation fixes are historical and already in the port.** Starlight’s 2025 commits [`7b7231b`](https://github.com/ss14Starlight/space-station-14/commit/7b7231bca99d1f78053d81cc11a87500cce10334) and [`976425c`](https://github.com/ss14Starlight/space-station-14/commit/976425c04e72d02e175df7a04939464a5781da70) fixed shared animations and invisibility. Wolfgate already has per-entity animation events and networked visibility in its Stasis system/component.
- **Tongue-based speech requires missing infrastructure.** [Starlight PR #5999](https://github.com/ss14Starlight/space-station-14/pull/5999) moves allowed emotes and voice sets to `OrganTongue` during surgery and adds a vocal cyber-tongue. Wolfgate currently assigns speech and voice directly to the Avali body and has no `OrganTongue` implementation/prototypes, so this is a larger surgery and speech-system port.
- **Other audited improvements:** [PR #4159](https://github.com/ss14Starlight/space-station-14/pull/4159) fixes Avali inventory species-ID casing; Wolfgate already uses `speciesId: avali`. [PR #5515](https://github.com/ss14Starlight/space-station-14/pull/5515) changes ProtoAvali female jumpsuit displacement and glove displacement, not the base Avali. No base-Avali organ-size, feet, or marking fix was found in the reviewed merged PRs.

## Appearance-layer note

Starlight’s current Avali prototype opts into hiding `Tail` and `HeadTop` through `HumanoidAppearance`. Wolfgate’s [HideLayerClothingSystem](../../../Content.Shared/Clothing/EntitySystems/HideLayerClothingSystem.cs) applies that list only when equipped clothing also maps the same layer to the worn slot in its `HideLayerClothingComponent`. The humanoid list alone does not hide layers under every helmet or suit. Treat this as a candidate only after checking which Wolfgate clothing definitions request those layers and verifying that `HeadTop` matches the Avali crest visual layer.
