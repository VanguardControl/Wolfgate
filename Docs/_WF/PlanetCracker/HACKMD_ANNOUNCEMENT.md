# Planet Cracking is coming to Wolfgate

[TOC]

## The pitch

You buy the most expensive ship in the sector. You park it in orbit over a planet. Then you rip a chunk of that planet out of the ground, hang it in front of your bridge and strip mine it.

That's the feature. Everything below is how it goes wrong.

## The ship

The **Planet Cracker**. Capital class. Slow. Defensive guns only. VERY expensive, we're talking 3 million, one per round.

It's a mining rig with a hull around it. Two gravity projectors bolted to the front, a centrifuge the size of a room in the middle, and a little transport shuttle docked on the side. It ships with two gravity anchors in crates. You will learn to hate those crates.

## How a crack goes

**1. Pick a planet.**
The bridge has a survey console. It lists every planet in the sector, what it roughly contains, whether it's been cracked already, and whether cracking it is legal. More on that last part later.

**2. Park in orbit.**
Planets get a real orbit. You FTL in, you sit above the planet, you can see the surface below you. So can anyone else who turns up.

**3. Survey.**
Take the transport down. Walk the surface with the surveyor. It shows you the deep veins, the stuff you can't reach with a pickaxe. That's what you're here for.

**4. Anchors.**
The anchors are massive. The transport can carry ONE at a time and if you try to cheat it the shuttle drops out of the sky. Two trips. Drag each one into position, wrench it down. When both are in range of each other a circle gets drawn on the ground between them. That circle is your chunk.

Then you start the drills. Five minutes each.

**5. Things crawl out.**
The drilling cracks the ground around each anchor and stuff comes up through the cracks. Not once. In waves, for the whole five minutes, and they go for the anchors first. Bring people with guns.

**6. Spin up.**
Back on the ship. Spin the centrifuge to full. Target the anchors on the crack console. Hit begin.

From this second on you are in the **fall stage**. The ship locks in place. If the centrifuge drops below full spin, or either projector loses power, you have five minutes to fix it. If you don't, the ship and everything attached to it falls onto the planet and explodes. There is no undo.

**7. The crack.**
Two beams from the projectors down to the anchors. The whole ship shakes for ten to twenty minutes depending on how big a circle you drew and how good your projector parts are. On the surface a ring of cracks spreads around the circle. When it's done the circle tears out of the ground and appears in front of your bridge, hanging in orbit. Anyone standing on it goes up with it. A hole is left behind on the planet.

**8. Mine.**
Wrench crack miners onto the chunk over the veins. No atmosphere out there so suit up. It's a LOT of ore.

**9. Let go.**
When you're done, go onto the chunk and switch both anchors off within a minute of each other. Then you have one minute to get off it. It falls back into the hole and explodes.

One crack per planet per round.

## The bits I'm most excited about

- **Orbit is a real place.** Other ships can FTL into the same orbit, see you on radar, dock, board you, shoot you. A cracker mid-crack can't move. Think about what that means for pirates.
- **The fall.** No scripted death. The ship is held up by the centrifuge and when that goes, physics does the rest. You'll watch the surface get closer.
- **The console.** No text walls. A top-down diagram of the site with your anchors and the circle, a centrifuge dial that visibly spins with the real spin, the beams drawn live, the countdowns in red when something's failing.
- **The beams.** Projector to anchor, across the gap between orbit and surface. Visible from both ends.
- **Sanctioned planets.** Some planets are legal to crack. Some are not. Crack an unsanctioned one and the whole sector gets told who you are and where you are. The TSF can come for you. Unsanctioned planets pay out FAR more. Your call.
- **Something is buried on the unsanctioned ones.** Goodluck.

## Status

Design is done and it's built on systems Wolfgate already has, which is why this is actually happening and not a wishlist. The planets you already see in the sector, procedural planet surfaces with ore and hostiles, z-levels, grids falling and crashing, gravity generators with real capacity, all in the codebase today. The big missing piece is giving those planets an orbit you can sit in and a surface you can drop to, which is first on the list.
