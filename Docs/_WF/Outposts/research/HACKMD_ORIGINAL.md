Outposts is the next iteration of planet implementation, and it brings fourth the final stages of the whole cycle. Think RIMWORLD.

Outposts aims to introduce buildable outposts, as well as spawning outposts, on planets. 

Planets will now be a set size instead of being infinite, allowing for "story generation" style of random gen, where there are specific POI's on the map with beacons that players can venture to. These POI's will range from ruins, trade posts all the way to heavenly shrines and alien artifact sites.

Players will have the option to build and save their outpost, those that wish to start a new one will have to purchase the "Planet Outpost Kit" containing the required building blocks to start their own outpost. The most important part being the "Outpost Console", the outpost version of the shuttle console, and what determines that the grid is actually an outpost, as well as being the command center for all outpost functions.

Outposts will be the first version of our "shipsaving" system, where players will be able to put their Outpost Console down and save from within the console. 

After a player has saved their first outpost, a new option to spawn will be available, named "Spawn at Outpost" which will load their saved outpost and place them inside it, as long as they have an "Outpost Crypod" built. More on that in the spawn options feature.

Outposts will have the ability for access control, default being the players access. (see ship access overhaul, outposts get the same feature)

Outposts are ANCHORED and cannot be moved, even with thrusters put on them, unless you destroy the outpost console, in which case they can be.

Players can only own ONE OUTPOST AT A TIME PER ROUND.

You can set the name of your outpost.

## Outpost Saving / Loading
Outposts can be saved at any time via the Outpost Console, and will be autosaved every 10 minutes, creating an autosave file. 

You save/load your outpost in the save tab of the outpost console, saved outposts are linked to your current character and cannot be transferred. You can view your characters saved outpost in the character customiser menu. A character can have upto 3 outposts saved, plus 1 autosave. You can delete these in the save load menu.

This tab will show you a preview of your outpost(like the ship previewer)

### Loading a Saved Outpost
In order to load your previously saved outpost you must first place down the outpost console that you can purchase, and then navigate to the Save tab and click Load, each outpost save file is price calculated on the sum of the value of all of the parts + 50%, when you press load, it is deducted from your account(if you have enough funds). This then checks the area for any non-planetary turfs(to prevent griefing by loading over other grids) and ensures it's safe to load, and then gives you a 30 second warning to clear the area(a ghost overlay shows) and then loads the grid, the outpost console being exactly where it is in your saved grid, thus being the "anchor point".

There will also be some preset base game outposts that you can spawn, for their set price. Or you can just build your own from scratch.

## Spawn Options
We will now give players the option to start the round in, which overrides how they spawn into the round entirely.

There will be a spawn menu that shows allowing you to configure each spawn type(see options for each spawn option). If it is pre-round, you will be able to select and set up your Outpost/Shuttle Crash scenarios.

Spawn at outpost shows your available to play outposts plus the base maps to start with, costing the amount from your bank account.

### Multiplay Spawn Options
You will notice that some spawn options have Multiplay, this is a new feature where you can set up your play group to be joinable pre-game, meaning a player can configure themselves to join your outpost/crash landed situation at roundstart if you allow it. You can also invite a player to join. They'll get setup with the same access to doors and such. 

You can create Play Groups that are essentially sub-lobbies of the game, that players can join to be part of your multiplay group, you can set it to be Open / Join Code / Private(Requests are sent to you as a notification, pre-roundstart, and you can directly invite a connected player)

In the Outpost Configurator you can also setup joinable positions for your outpost, which again, other players can join, they'll spawn at the outpost cryopods.

For the crash landed start, players can only join your play group at the lobby start, where they'll spawn on the ship with you.

### Currently Proposed Spawn Options:
1. Standard - Normal spawning as their selected job. (Difficulty: Normal) (Options: Role)
2. Shuttle Crash - You spawn in a randomly selected shuttle, crash landing on a randomly selected world, as a randomly selected job. There is a crash landing sequence on the ship, the console cannot be controlled while in this sequence, the PA system announces that the shuttle will crash in 3 minutes(time you have to prepare) and then enter atmosphere. The shuttle spawns in the orbit layer of the planet. (Difficulty: Very Hard). The player then has to scrounge up materials to escape the planet. Spawning has to take into account nearby POI's and other players outposts as to spawn far enough away from these to not impact those things. (Options: Multiplay)
3. Outpost Spawn - You spawn on your outpost as your Outpost Job(a custom job that you create for yourself in the outpost console, which allows you to select the starting jumpsuit etc, basically the loadout menu). The outpost is then spawned on the planet you saved it on, deducting the price of the outpost from your balance, and rejecting if you don't have enough. (Options: Selected Outpost to Spawn at, Multiplay). 


## Ship Access Overhaul
Ships will now have an access options panel in the shuttle console, a bought ship/outpost will default to ONLY the players access UNLESS they are part of a faction, in which case, the shuttle spawns with the faction access as normal. The owner of the non-faction vessel can add specific people to the access list, or set an "Access Code" which gates doors behind that code. Each door on the shuttle can be individually configured as well(as shown on the access diagram which is just a wire diagram showing selectable doors). Meaning you can set a specific door to a specific player/code. To open a code-locked door when you don't have specific access, you simple right click on the door > Enter Code, and a code UI shows to enter the 4 digit code, opening the door.

Outpost Consoles will be different, these are only accessible to the outpost owner. You can sell your outpost to other players as well. Outposts can be sold at round-end as well, or mid-round in the console(same shuttle sell checks occur) for the exact calculated value of the outpost at the time.

## Outpost Landing Pads
Currently, the only way to land on a planet is using ships capable of landing on the planet.

Outpost landing pads allow you to designate an area of your outpost where a shuttle can land without the requirement of lift, they can only land at this pad and take off from it, and they have to be roughly above it. It acts as a docking port essentially(in game will look like a small staircase with railings).

## Outpost Trade Panel
Outposts will be given a trade panel in their console that, assuming you have a cargo area set(done by placing down the supplied Universal Trade Hub supplied by either your new outpost or the base one), that will allow the player to BUY(this is a buy only hub) outpost specific supplies and gizmos(see below). These gizmos are then parachuded in to the UTH.

## Farming
Given planets have their own fauna and wildlife, you will be able to farm animals and such, using breeding/animal herding mechanics that will allow you to capture these animals and use them to produce things. Requires an animal pen and such.

## Planetary Points of Interest
Planets will have a plethora of POI's to investigate, things like mines, abandoned outposts, automated outposts, tradeposts, derelict capital ships, smol derelicts, farms, workshops, etc. 

You will be able to claim these POI's as your outpost if you don't have one already. Each one will have an outpost console to interact with, if applicable.

## Outpost Attacks
Outposts will draw the attention of nearby settlers and fauna, these will be NPC's that will attack your base at night/whenever the planets "Active" state is. There will be a gizmo to give you a few minutes notice of an incoming attack. These attacks can range from a rabid rabbit all the way to a raiding group of raiders(all NPC controlled). It is recommended that you equip your outpost with defence turrets.

Depending on the attack type, these nasties will come from burrows, shuttles, vehicles.

## Jetpacks in the Atmosphere
Atmospheric Jetpacks will be able to transit through the atmosphere levels, normal jetpacks are useless. They'll use welding fuel to work. Some planets have super strong gravity, no workies.
