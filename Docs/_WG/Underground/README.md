# Underground

The Underground Black Market as a faction. So far this is policy and guidebook text only: Conflict Rule 7 names the
Underground a neutral faction under the Godfather, makes its TSF bounty a live capture with bail and a one-hour
lockout, treats its station as an outpost the war level never opens, and says what it can be hired for. The other
rules, the PDV standing orders, the faction's guidebook page and its job and lobby text are edited to agree, along
with the rules a PDV heretic mark falls under.

Players read it in the guidebook under Rules and Factions. No mechanics have changed: the bounties, the heretic mark
and the station are as they were.
