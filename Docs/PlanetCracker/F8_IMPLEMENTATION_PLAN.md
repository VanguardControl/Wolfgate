## OPEN RISKS
- THE 3x3 ANCHOR DEFEATS BOTH NPC MELEE RANGE GATES. An edge-adjacent mob is 2.0 tiles from WFGravityAnchor's transform origin (its fixture is a 3x3 PhysShapeAabb, anchors.yml:52-63). TargetInRangePrecondition (TargetInRangePrecondition.cs:33) against MeleeRange 1f (NPCBlackboard.cs:30) and NPCCombatSystem.Attack's `distance > weapon.Range` against 1.5f (NPCCombatSystem.Melee.cs:98, MeleeWeaponComponent.cs:98) are both centre-to-centre and neither is ever met. The anchor damage lands ONLY through the NavSmash pathfinding obstacle branch (NPCSteeringSystem.Obstacles.cs:162-194), which has no range check. F8 accepts this and does not widen either range. The visible cost is HTN plan churn every 0.45 s per mob camped against an anchor it can never 'reach'.
- NPCs SLEEP WHEN NOBODY IS ON THE SURFACE, so 'it drills unattended for 5 minutes ... the anchors need defending' (PLANET_CRACKER_DESIGN.md:61) does not happen unmodified. npc.pause_when_no_players_in_range defaults true with npc.player_pause_distance 32 (Content.Shared/CCVar/CCVars.NPC.cs:25/:30) and CheckPlayerDistancesAndPauseNPCs (NPCSystem.cs:174-230) measures with EntityCoordinates.TryDistance, which fails across maps - crew on the cracker hull four z-layers up are on a different map. F8 leaves the default. The one-line escape hatch, if the design later wants an unattended siege, is HTNComponent.SleepPlayerCheckRangeOverride (HTNComponent.cs:57) set at stamp time, at real CPU cost.
- F8 MOBS ARE NEVER CLEANED UP. Directly-spawned mobs are not in BiomeComponent.LoadedEntities so UnloadEntities never deletes them (BiomeSystem.ChunkLoader.cs:233-275), and UnloadTiles empties the ground under any mob standing off a pinned tile (:277-303), dropping it a z-layer. The cumulative Cap of 15 per anchor plus the separate surge budget are the only things bounding them for the round. Decide separately whether abandoned sites should be swept when the cracker leaves.
- THE PIN IS PERMANENT AND SUPPRESSES MARKERS. BiomeSystem.WFChunkPin.cs ships no unpin. Every pinned fissure tile permanently stops biome regeneration on it (ForEachTileInChunk's guard at ChunkLoader.cs:38-42) and permanently suppresses ore and F2 deep-vein markers on it in any marker chunk not yet generated (MarkerProcessor.cs:281). F8 caps the exposure at <=30 tiles per anchor with tile-exact ReserveTiles calls, but it is irreversible.
- FISSURE DECALS INSIDE THE CUT CIRCLE ARE DESTROYED AT EXTRACTION, not carried up: TryExtract stamps the disc Tile.Empty (Extraction.cs:169) and DecalSystem.OnTileChanged deletes every decal on those tiles (DecalSystem.cs:165-206). D19 'persists for the round' is true only for fissures outside the disc. The design does not say which it meant.
- fissure.rsi DECLARES directions: 4 ON fissure-1..4 BUT DecalOverlay DRAWS Frame0 ONLY (Content.Client/Decals/Overlays/DecalOverlay.cs:97), so three quarters of each sheet is unreachable through the decal path and per-decal variety comes only from TryAddDecal's Angle argument. F5 hit the identical issue on crack_rim and deliberately left the meta.json alone (F5_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md:450) because changing directions without re-cutting the pngs fails RSI validation. Accept one visual per stage or commission single-direction pngs; do not edit meta.json.
- HALF THE SHIPPED FACTIONS CANNOT DAMAGE THE ANCHOR. Cultists, Mercenaries, Explorers, Punks and most Silicons parent MobHumanoidHostileAIComplex whose NavSmash is commented out (Resources/Prototypes/_NF/Entities/Mobs/NPCs/mob_hostile_base.yml:350-351), and Carps fly on FlyingMobMask = Impassable|HighImpassable (CollisionGroup.cs:43) which does not overlap the anchor's MidImpassable|LowImpassable fixture layer. Assigning any of those to a planet silently turns 'the anchors need defending' into 'the players need defending'. Asclepiu ships Xenos and Argocytes for this reason; any new wfPlanetSurface must be checked the same way.
- CORRECTED (review issues 6 and 13): THE ANCHOR IS A CANDIDATE TARGET ONLY INSIDE THE MOB'S VISION RADIUS, NOT MAP-WIDE. The union at NpcFactionSystem.cs:193 is indeed unfiltered by range, but WFFissureTargets includes TargetInLOSOrCurrentCon, which NPCUtilitySystem.cs:358-374 evaluates as `_examine.InRangeUnOccluded(owner, target, radius + 0.5f)` - a hard 0 beyond the blackboard vision radius unless the anchor is already the current Target within it - and the scoring loop drops any candidate whose running score hits 0 (:153-160). The consequence cuts the OPPOSITE way to the reviewed plan's note: a mob knocked back or lured away will NOT return to the anchor by itself, so D14's 'mobs target the anchor first' holds only locally, around the spawn site. The DeAggroEntity mitigation (Exception.cs:112) is therefore unnecessary and F8 does not call it; if the design later wants a genuine siege, raise the stamped mobs' vision radius on the blackboard rather than removing the consideration.
- THE SURGE NOW RESOLVES ITS OWN CONTEXT, so a hand-spawned or admin-driven pair surges correctly, but that also means the surge fires for an anchor whose drill never ran (any `wfcracker complete drill` site). That is the intended reading of D14 ('a final surge at extraction'), but it is a behaviour change from the reviewed design, which would silently have surged nothing. If a future decision makes the surge conditional on a real drill, gate it on comp.SpawnedTotal > 0 rather than on comp.Armed, which is false by then either way.
- THE MOB ROLL RE-ROLLS AT MOST MobRollRetries TIMES. With a pathological faction whose every group can roll empty, a granted slot is still abandoned after five attempts and the ring quietly spawns fewer mobs than the design's 2-4. The shipped tables cannot hit that (every faction has at least one fixed-amount group), but a future wfPlanetSurface pointing at a custom table can. The cap is never consumed by an abandoned slot, so the loss is per-ring, not per-anchor.
