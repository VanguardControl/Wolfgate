[color=#a4885c][head=4]Wolfgate Rules[/head][/color]

[color=#a4885c][head=3]Foreword[/head][/color]

[italic]Wolfgate is a derivative of [bold]Monolith[/bold], and our ruleset is based on theirs.[/italic]

Servers are often a diluted mess of rules and restrictions that hinder players' creative abilities and freedom to craft their own experiences.

We do expect, at the very least, that you [bold]stay in-character, adhere to the setting, and engage in quality roleplay to the best of your abilities[/bold].

This ensures your enjoyment and enhances the experience for the other players around you.

[bold]Please make sure to also read the lore primer to better understand your character's circumstances and context to the round.[/bold]

[color=#a4885c][head=4]Rule List[/head][/color]

All of the below rules apply at all times, including between rounds, in-game, and elsewhere in our community.

[color=red][italic]Read the full rules and lore primer in the guidebook on the escape or (Numpad 0) menus.[/italic][/color]
[color=red][italic]These rules are expected to be read in their entirety.[/italic][/color]

[color=#a4885c][head=3]Core Rules[/head][/color]
These are the core rules of Wolfgate:
	• 0. Players must be, and act, 18 or above to play Wolfgate.
	• 1. Use common sense.
	• 2. Respect each other.
	• 3. Do not engage in cheating or exploits.
	• 4. Do not use alternative accounts.
	• 5. All communication must be in English.
	• 6. Do not abuse the Admin Help System.
	• 7. Do not threaten to report a player.

[color=#a4885c][head=3]Roleplay Rules[/head][/color]
These are the roleplay rules of Wolfgate:
	• 1. Do not break character.
	• 2. Avoid misusing in-character text chat.
	• 3. Type with proper grammar in in-character text chat.
	• 4. Do not name yourself well known people or things.
	• 5. Do not metagame.
	• 6. Safe Zones
	• 7. Do not abandon your faction role.
	• 8. Commanders have higher standards.

[color=#a4885c][head=3]Conflict Rules[/head][/color]
These are the conflict rules of Wolfgate:
	• 1. Avoid random deathmatch.
	• 2. Adhere to the new life rule.
	• 3. Engage in fair play when making demands.
	• 4. Follow the conditions for portstriking.

[color=#a4885c][head=3]Admin Decisions[/head][/color]
	• [color=yellow][bold]All admin decisions are final.[/bold][/color]
	• Rules are enforced to the [color=yellow]spirit of the rules[/color] and subject to admin interpretation.
	• Admins may disregard any/all rules if they deem it in the best interest of the current round, the server, and/or the community.
	• Admins are accountable for their actions. Abuse of admin privileges will be dealt with by staff.

[color=#a4885c][head=3]General Information[/head][/color]
	• Disconnecting from or ignoring/evading admin-helps will result in an [color=red]appeal-only ban[/color].
	• Admins are not obligated to cover player losses, deaths or delays.
		• This includes, but is not limited to: ship deeds, money, respawns and revivals.
		• Any action taken is at the staff's discretion, and depends on the circumstances.
