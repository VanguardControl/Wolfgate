# Roofs

Roofs you can't see through. The open floor of every roofed tile gets a solid roof drawn over it after lighting, lit
by the map's daylight and shaded along its edges: a vessel or station's enclosed rooms as hull plating (exterior
plating, catwalks and lattice open to the outside stay bare), a planet building's roofed floor as roofing. Walls,
windows, doors and other full-height structures are never covered, so from outside a building shows its walls and
doors as normal with roof only over its rooms. What stands on each tile is remembered and looked at again whenever the
tile or anything anchored on it changes, so building, deconstruction and breaches show at once. Caverns and other
layers under a world layer are left alone, as their roof is the ground above, and so is the ship previewer.

The roof over whatever the local player is in lifts: aboard a ship its whole roof, in a building the room they stand
in, filled out through open doors and stopped by walls, windows and shut doors (`WFRoofReveal`). A ghost, an admin
ghost or an observer sees no roofs, nor the roof shade that keeps daylight out of buildings and ships (upstream
`RoofOverlay`); underground layers keep their shade.

Entry points: `WFRoofSystem` (what is covered and what lifts), `WFRoofOverlay` (the drawing).
